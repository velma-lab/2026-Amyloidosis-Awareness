A Daughter's Vow

Why I Started This Campaign

Bishop Bernita Rivers Smalls, Ph.D. was my mother, my pastor, and my best friend. On August 22, 2025, amyloidosis took her from us — a disease her medical providers never identified, because too few physicians know to look for it.

"A treatment existed. My mother simply never had the chance to receive it, because no one knew to look for the disease."

Amyloidosis disproportionately affects Black Americans, yet it remains one of the most overlooked diseases in modern medicine. Every symptom dismissed as "just getting older" is a chance for an earlier diagnosis lost.

"I can't," I cried, "let this happen to another family."

That vow is why this foundation exists — so that no other family experiences what mine did: watching someone they love fight the wrong battle because the right diagnosis came too late.

— Rev. Dr. Elveria Glover, Founder
